Two sources report different numbers. The gap is real and fully explained below.
NEAR RPC: Gross Wallet Inflows
$16.71M
Every token entering the buyback wallet, including solver settlements that transit and are rebated back out during execution
DefiLlama: Net Protocol Revenue (this model)
$8.71M
Protocol revenue after solver rebates, the permanent keep. Basis for the 5.91 bps capture rate and all offset calculations above
Gap: $8.0M (ratio 1.92x). Solvers settle through the buyback wallet during execution; the net rebate flows back out. NEAR RPC counts gross; DefiLlama counts net. Using the gross figure would overstate the capture rate by nearly 2x and produce a falsely optimistic break-even. This model uses DefiLlama throughout.

The settlement contract collects roughly 0.01 bps per side, hundreds of times below the 5.91 bps revenue rate the break-even model uses. Protocol revenue is therefore generated off the settlement contract, through application-layer fees or solver arrangements. The capture rate is policy, not protocol: it can be changed without governance, in either direction. This strengthens the case that a contract-enforced fee switch is the decisive catalyst.
